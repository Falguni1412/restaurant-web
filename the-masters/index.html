Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

Born from a passion that spans three generations, The Masters is more than a restaurant — it is a tribute to the timeless traditions of Indian cuisine. Our founder, Chef Arjun Mehta, began his journey in the royal kitchens of Rajasthan, learning the secrets of spice from his grandmother.

Today, The Masters stands as a beacon of authentic Indian fine dining, where every dish tells a story of heritage, every spice is hand-ground, and every meal becomes a cherished memory.

Master Chef

Chef Arjun Mehta

Founder & Executive Chef

With over 25 years of culinary mastery, Chef Arjun has trained under legendary chefs across India, France, and Japan. His philosophy combines ancient techniques with modern presentation, creating dishes that are both authentic and innovative.

A recipient of the prestigious "Chef of the Year" award by the Indian Culinary Foundation, Chef Arjun continues to push boundaries while honoring the timeless traditions of Indian cuisine.
